Privacy Policy

Published by Riniso · Effective 5 October 2026 · App ID com.riniso.qvoice

In short

  • QVoice collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of its own.
  • All speech is made on your phone. Text you give QVoice never leaves your device.
  • The reader keeps only the last text you opened, on your phone, so you can continue listening later.

Text QVoice reads

  • As your phone's text-to-speech engine. Apps such as screen readers, e-book readers or maps send QVoice text to speak. QVoice turns it into audio on your phone and keeps none of it.
  • In QVoice's reader. Text you choose Read aloud for in another app, share to QVoice (including .txt files), paste, or open with the "Read copied text" shortcut is shown and read aloud on your phone.
  • The last text and your place in it are kept in QVoice's private storage on your phone, so reading can continue after QVoice was closed or the phone restarted. Only that one text is kept. It is replaced when you open another text, and deleted when you tap "Read something else" in the reader, clear QVoice's storage, or uninstall QVoice. It is never sent anywhere and is never part of a backup.
  • Notifications and the lock screen show only where the reader is (for example "Paragraph 3 of 12"), never the text.
  • Your clipboard is read only when you ask for it: the Paste button in the reader and the "Read copied text" shortcut. Android shows a notice whenever an app reads the clipboard. The shortcut doesn't read aloud copied text marked as sensitive, such as a password copied from a password manager.

Network use

QVoice uses the internet only when you choose to download a voice. Voice files come from GitHub (github.com and its file servers), where the voices' publishers host them, through Android's download manager. As with any website visit, GitHub receives your IP address and the standard technical details of the request; GitHub's privacy statement applies to that. QVoice sends no identifiers of its own. Downloads use HTTPS and are checked against known checksums before they are installed.

Stored on your phone

Your settings (default voices, licences you have accepted, the reading speed, the processor setting and how fast each voice runs on your phone), the voices you download, and the reader's last text and place. You can delete downloaded voices in QVoice, and everything by clearing QVoice's storage or uninstalling it. If Android backup is on, your voice choices, reading speed and accepted licences are included in your device backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Downloaded voices, the processor setting and the reader's text are never backed up.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download the voices you choose.
  • Network state: to ask before a large download over mobile data.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep reading aloud while the screen is off or you use another app, with its notification.
  • Prevent phone from sleeping: so the phone doesn't fall asleep in the middle of reading aloud with the screen off.

None of these give QVoice access to your personal data, and QVoice asks for no permission that would.

If you contact us

When you email us, for example with "Send feedback" in the app, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. The feedback email starts with the app version, the Android version, the phone model and which text-to-speech engine the phone uses; you see these in your email app before sending and can delete them. We don't share your emails with anyone, and we delete them on request.

Children

QVoice is a general-audience tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Open source

QVoice is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Anyone can check what it does in its source code: github.com/cyber-cyper/qvoice.
